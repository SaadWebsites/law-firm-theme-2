04Common questions

Family Law FAQ

How is custody decided in California? +
Courts focus on the best interests of the child, considering stability, the health of the child and each parent’s relationship with the child.
Do we have to go to court? +
No. Many families resolve matters through mediation or negotiation. We help you try that route first.
How much child support will be ordered? +
California uses a guideline formula based on income, timeshare and other factors. We can give you a close estimate.
