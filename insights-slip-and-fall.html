Slip and fall claims in California, explained.

A fall on someone else’s property can be a serious, compensable injury. Here is how the law works.

By the attorneys at Harlan & Voss LLP · 4 minute read

What you must prove

A premises liability claim generally requires that the property owner owed you a duty of care, breached it, and that the breach caused your injury and losses.

Notice is usually the battleground

Owners are responsible for hazards they created or knew about, and sometimes for hazards they should have discovered through reasonable inspection. Evidence of how long a hazard existed is often decisive.

Do not let the business control the narrative

Report the incident, get the report number, photograph the hazard and gather witness information. Avoid recorded statements to insurers.

The deadline

Most slip and fall claims must be filed within two years, and against government property in as little as six months.
