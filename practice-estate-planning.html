04Common questions

Wills, Trusts & Estates FAQ

Do I need a trust or is a will enough? +
It depends on your assets, goals and whether you want to avoid probate. We explain the trade offs in plain language and you decide.
What is probate? +
The court supervised process of settling an estate. It can be slow and costly, which is why many clients plan to avoid it.
Can you help if a will is being contested? +
Yes. We represent executors, trustees, heirs and beneficiaries in estate and trust disputes.
