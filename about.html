01Our story

Two decades in downtown Los Angeles.

What began as a two attorney litigation shop on Spring Street has grown into a full service firm at 445 S Figueroa, serving clients from across Los Angeles County and Southern California.

We deliberately kept the firm boutique. Our caseload is small enough that every client works directly with an attorney, yet deep enough that we have tried dozens of cases to verdict and handled a wide range of complex matters. That balance, personal attention with real courtroom experience, is what our clients value most.
