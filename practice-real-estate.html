04Common questions

Real Estate FAQ

Do I need a lawyer to buy a home? +
A broker handles the deal, but a lawyer protects your legal interest, especially when title, disclosures or financing issues arise.
Can you help evict a tenant? +
Yes, we advise landlords and tenants on the lawful process and represent them when matters escalate.
What is a title dispute? +
A conflict over who legally owns a property or a portion of it, often from old deeds, boundary errors or inheritance issues.
